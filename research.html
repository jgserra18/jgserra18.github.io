Research

Current Research

My research examines how nitrogen is used, lost, and governed in agricultural systems, and how these dynamics constrain sustainable food production. I combine field observations, process-based modelling and machine-learning to link nitrogen inputs, crop productivity and environmental losses across scales.

A defining feature of my work is bridging bottom-up evidence with top-down assessments to expose overlooked nitrogen inputs, reduce uncertainty, and develop indicators that are both agronomically credible and policy-relevant. Substantively, I focus on nitrogen use efficiency, nitrate leaching and nitrous oxide emissions, and the role of management practices and crop structure in shaping sustainable nitrogen boundaries.
