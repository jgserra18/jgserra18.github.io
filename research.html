Research

Key Scientific Findings

  • Irrigation water is a systematic and overlooked nitrogen input in agriculture.
  • Current nitrate vulnerable zones in Europe fail to capture major groundwater pollution hotspots.
  • Large areas of cropland operate outside agronomically viable and environmentally safe nitrogen boundaries.
  • Scientific effort is misaligned with nitrogen pollution severity across global regions.

Current Research

My research examines how nitrogen is used, lost, and governed in agricultural systems, and how these dynamics constrain sustainable food production. I combine field observations, process-based modelling and machine-learning to link nitrogen inputs, crop productivity and environmental losses across scales.

I bridge bottom-up evidence with top-down assessments to expose overlooked nitrogen inputs, reduce uncertainty, and develop indicators that are both agronomically credible and policy-relevant. I focus on nitrogen use efficiency, nitrate leaching, nitrous oxide emissions, and the role of management practices and crop structure in shaping sustainable nitrogen boundaries.

Selected Past Projects

These projects underpin my current research agenda and reflect a consistent focus on nitrogen balances, environmental losses, and policy-relevant indicators across scales:

INMS - The International Nitrogen Management System

Established regional and global barriers to nitrogen management through comprehensive analysis, contributing to the International Nitrogen Assessment with evidence-based policy recommendations.

AgroGreen-SUDOE - Transnational cooperation for the design of more sustainable agricultural production strategies in the SUDOE Territory.

Quantified overlooked nitrogen input via irrigation water using AquaCrop and GlobWat models across Portugal/Spain/Europe.

NitroPortugal H2020 - Strengthening Portuguese Research and Innovation Capacities in the Field of Excessive Reactive Nitrogen

Mapped cross-scale nitrogen fluxes in agricultural landscapes across Portugal to identify pollution hotspots and management opportunities.
