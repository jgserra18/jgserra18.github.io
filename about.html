Background

My research journey in nitrogen science began through interdisciplinary opportunities that spanned from field measurements to global assessments, leading to extensive work tracking nitrogen compounds across scales.

Interests

You can find me actively reading a book outside (if the sun allows it), bouldering (if my knees allow it), working out in the gym or just jamming to some jazz-techno-hip hop music.

Philosophy

My research aims to improve nitrogen use efficiency and enhance evidence uptake in policy translation within the agri-environmental sector. I support open, transparent and reproducible science via the PRISMA guidelines.
