Background

I did not choose nitrogen, but I think nitrogen chose me. My life is a story of how the stars aligned and I ended up spending thousand of hours tracking the different nitrogen compounds across the world, ranging from the field to the global scales.

Interests

You can find me actively reading a book outside (if the sun allows it), bouldering (if my knees allow it), working out in the gym or just jamming to some jazz-techno-hip hop music.

Philosophy

My research aims to not only contribute to improving nitrogen use efficiency, but also to a higher knowledge use efficiency in the agri-environmental sector. I support open, transparent and reproducible science via the PRISMA guidelines.
