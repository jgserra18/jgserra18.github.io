Profile

Current Position

Postdoctoral researcher (2024–present) at the Center for Landscape Research in Sustainable Agricultural Futures (Land-CRAFT) and the Department of Agroecology, Aarhus University, Denmark.

My work focuses on farm- and landscape-scale nitrogen modelling, combining process-based models and data-driven approaches. This includes the development and application of LandscapeDNDC and FarmAC within integrated FarmScape modelling frameworks to assess nitrogen use efficiency, nitrate leaching, and greenhouse gas emissions.

Skills & Expertise

  • Nitrogen cycling, nitrogen use efficiency, and sustainable nutrient management
  • Process-based biogeochemical modelling (LandscapeDNDC, FarmAC, AquaCrop)
  • Integration of field data, spatial datasets, and machine-learning approaches
  • Science–policy interface, nitrogen indicators, and policy-relevant assessments
